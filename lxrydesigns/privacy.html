1. Who we are

The data controller for this website is LXRY Designs Ltd (registered in England & Wales, company no. 14265179), registered office 17 Victoria Road East, Thornton Cleveleys, FY5 5HT, United Kingdom, operating from Divonne-les-Bains, France.

For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact us at hello@lxrydesigns.com.

2. What data we collect

This is a simple marketing website. We keep data collection to a minimum:

  • When you contact us (by email, telephone or WhatsApp): your name, contact details and whatever you choose to put in your message. The site's contact buttons open your own email, phone or WhatsApp app — we only receive what you send us.
  • Technical/server data: our host records standard server logs (including IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site.
  • Language preference: if you switch between English and French, your choice is stored locally in your browser (not sent to us).

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and there is no tracking pixel or advertising network on this site.

3. Why we use it and our legal basis

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide our services — on the basis of steps taken at your request / our legitimate interest in answering you.
  • To operate and secure the website — on the basis of our legitimate interest in running a safe, functioning site.

We keep enquiry correspondence for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless we enter into a project together (in which case we keep records for as long as needed for the engagement and any legal or accounting obligations). Server logs are retained for a short period by our host.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you are based (UK GDPR, EU GDPR and applicable Swiss law), you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, email hello@lxrydesigns.com.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO); in France, the CNIL; or your local authority.

7. Cookies

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. It uses your browser's local storage only to remember your language choice — a functional preference you can clear at any time in your browser settings.
