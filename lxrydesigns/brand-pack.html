Logo & usage

Palette in practice

  • Primary — Bright Turquoise #27F4D2, used solid across the whole site (mark, wordmark, buttons, accents).
  • Deep accent — Tiffany Green #00A19B for subtle gradient depth only, never as the main colour.
  • Strapline — “Digital Solutions” in white for contrast.
  • Turquoise on black; design dark-first.

The three lockups

  • Mark — the solid-turquoise dot-mesh wave; the hero of the identity, use it large.
  • Horizontal — mark + wordmark for headers & wide spaces.
  • Stacked — mark over wordmark + strapline for footers & social.
  • Mono — one-colour turquoise, white, or black cutouts; favicon built from the solid mesh.

Look & feel

  • Dots are solid, uniform turquoise — no gradient (it disappears on dark).
