Why automate?

Most small businesses lose a full day a week to repetitive, manual work — chasing enquiries, copying data between tools, writing the same replies, building the same reports. AI won't run your business, but it will quietly take those jobs off your plate.

This kit is ten workflows we see save the most time, ranked by how quickly you can put them to work. Each one lists what it does, the kind of tools that do it, roughly how many hours it gives back, and the smallest first step. Pick one. Ship it this week. Then come back for the next.

One rule before you start

  • Keep a human in the loop for anything a customer sees or that touches money.
  • Only feed these tools the data they actually need — less is safer.
  • If it processes personal data, it's covered by GDPR / Swiss FDPIC. Get consent, log why, keep it minimal.

The 10 workflows

Quick wins first

01

Inbox triage & drafted replies

AI reads incoming email, sorts it by intent and urgency, and pre-writes on-brand replies for you to approve and send.

ToolsGmail / Outlook + an AI assistant, or Make/Zapier + a language model
Time back3–5 h/wk

Start: connect your inbox, write 3–4 reply templates, keep every send behind a human click.

02

Lead capture → CRM sync

Every enquiry — form, DM, email — is logged to your CRM automatically, enriched and deduplicated, so nothing slips.

ToolsWeb form + Make/Zapier → HubSpot / Notion / a spreadsheet
Time back2 h/wk

Start: map your form fields to the CRM once, add a dedupe step on email.

03

Quotes & invoices from a brief

Turn a one-line description into a formatted quote or invoice, ready to review and send.

ToolsDocument automation + Stripe / QuickBooks / your accounting tool
Time back2 h/wk

Start: build one template with variables (client, items, totals), wire it to your product list.

04

Meeting notes & action items

Calls are transcribed automatically, then distilled into decisions and a clean task list sent to the right people.

ToolsFathom / Otter / tl;dv (+ your task tool)
Time back2–3 h/wk

Start: add a notetaker to your recurring calls, forward its summary into your task board.

05

Content repurposing

One long piece — a post, a talk, a call — becomes several social posts and short-form video clips.

ToolsAn AI writer + a clip tool (Opus Clip / Descript)
Time back4 h/wk

Start: take your best recent piece, produce five outputs from it before creating anything new.

06

Reviews & reputation

Ask for a review at the exact right moment, watch new ones land, and draft replies to each.

ToolsAutomation platform + Google Business Profile
Time back1–2 h/wk

Start: trigger a review request a few days after a completed job or delivery.

07

Document data extraction

Pull structured data out of PDFs, receipts and forms straight into your tools — no more retyping.

ToolsAI OCR / document AI + your spreadsheet or database
Time back2 h/wk

Start: pick one document you handle every week and automate just that one.

08

Scheduling & reminders

Let people book you directly, and send automatic reminders and follow-ups so nobody no-shows.

ToolsCal.com / Calendly + an automation for reminders
Time back2 h/wk

Start: share one booking link, add a two-step reminder sequence.

09

Customer FAQ assistant

An on-brand assistant answers your most common questions instantly — on the site or on WhatsApp.

ToolsA chatbot trained on your own docs and FAQs
Time back3 h/wk

Start: write your top 20 questions and answers, feed them in, hand off anything unusual to a human.

10

Weekly reporting

Dashboards build themselves from your tools, so the numbers that matter are ready every Monday.

ToolsLooker Studio / Google Sheets + connectors
Time back1–2 h/wk

Start: choose five metrics you actually act on, ignore the rest.

Do it right

Automate without breaking GDPR

The short compliance checklist

  • Collect and share only the personal data each workflow truly needs.
  • Get clear consent, and record the lawful basis for using the data.
  • Keep a human reviewing anything customer-facing or financial.
  • Check where your AI tools store data — EU/Swiss hosting is the safer default.
  • Where required, disclose that AI is being used (the EU AI Act is coming into force).
  • Review AI outputs before they go out — you're accountable for them.
